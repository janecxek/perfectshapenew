Get in touch

Where is Perfect Shape Zurich located?

Our studio is at Bahnhofstrasse 94 (2nd floor), 8001 Zurich – just a few minutes’ walk from Zurich main station (Hauptbahnhof) and easy to reach by tram, train or on foot.

How can I book an appointment?

Fastest online – 24/7 via our booking system. You can also reach us by phone at +41 76 608 61 61, via WhatsApp or by email.

Who performs the treatments?

Diagnosis and treatments are performed by certified physicians and qualified specialists – including Dr. med. Roya Jeyrani and Agnieszka Jaggy, a certified laser safety officer.

What does a treatment cost?

Our prices page shows transparent guide prices for all treatments – e.g. laser hair removal from CHF 40, lip enhancement from CHF 160 or thread lifts from CHF 240. The final cost is confirmed after your individual consultation.

Can I reschedule or cancel an appointment?

Yes, free of charge up to 24 hours before your appointment. For late cancellations or no-shows we charge a fee of CHF 50.

How can I pay?

Payment is made directly after the treatment in cash, by card or with TWINT.
