What is a Thread Lift?

The thread lift is one of the most popular minimally invasive ways to tighten sagging facial contours without surgery. At Perfect Shape on Bahnhofstrasse in Zurich, every treatment is planned around your individual anatomy – from a gentle V-lift to a defined jawline or a reduced double chin.

Absorbable PDO/PLLA threads are placed through tiny entry points. Fine micro-barbs gently reposition the tissue, while the natural breakdown of the threads stimulates new collagen. The result: an immediate lifting effect combined with a lasting improvement in skin structure.

  • A natural lift without incisions or scars
  • Definition of cheeks, jawline and neck
  • Quick, outpatient, minimal downtime

Who it’s for

Indications

  • Sagging jowls
  • Loss of cheek volume
  • Jawline contouring
  • Mild neck laxity
  • Double chin & under-eye bags

Good to know

Contraindications

  • Acute skin infections
  • Pregnancy/breastfeeding
  • Blood clotting disorders
  • Unrealistic expectations

Final suitability is assessed by a physician.

How long does a thread lift last?

The lifting effect usually lasts between 18 and 24 months. While the threads dissolve after a few months, the effect remains visible much longer thanks to new collagen.

Is a thread lift painful?

The treatment is performed under local anaesthesia (cream or infiltration), so you usually only feel slight pressure. Most patients find it very tolerable.

How long is the downtime?

Usually just 1–3 days. Mild swelling or bruising can occur and typically fades quickly.
